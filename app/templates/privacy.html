Privacy

{{ APP_NAME }} has no accounts, so there is very little about you to hold. This page is the whole of it.

What is stored

  • A random session id in a cookie. It is how a room knows two requests came from the same person. It is not linked to an identity.
  • The songs you queue and the votes you cast, attached to that session id and to the room you are in.
  • A generated nickname so the queue is readable. You are never asked for a real one.
  • No email address, no password, no profile. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers. Every asset on this page is served from this server.
  • No audio files. Media is streamed through and deleted the moment a track finishes — nothing is retained after playback.

How long it is kept

  • Rooms and everything in them are deleted once nobody has used them for {{ room_idle_days }} days. A host can delete a room immediately at any time.
  • Temporary caches (track titles, stream addresses, presence) expire on their own within hours and can be cleared at any moment without losing anything.

Your host key

When you create a room you receive a host key. The server keeps only a hash of it, so it cannot be recovered or handed to anyone else — including you. Host rights otherwise ride on your session cookie; clearing site data means giving them up unless you kept the key.
